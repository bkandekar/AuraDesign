Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This is a general-purpose template. Please review it with a qualified professional before publishing, and update it to reflect your actual business practices.

1. Deposits

Deposits paid to begin a project are non-refundable once work has commenced, as they secure our time and resources for your project.

2. Cancellations before work begins

If a project is cancelled before any work has started, a full refund of the deposit may be issued at our discretion.

3. Cancellations mid-project

If a project is cancelled after work has begun, the client will be billed for work completed to date. Any remaining balance from the deposit, if applicable, may be refunded.

4. Completed projects

Once a website has been delivered, approved and launched by the client, the project is considered complete and is not eligible for a refund.

5. Third-party costs

Costs paid to third parties on your behalf (domains, hosting, plugins, licenses) are non-refundable regardless of project status.

6. Contact us

For any refund-related questions, please reach out to us at hello@webora.in.
