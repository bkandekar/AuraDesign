Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

1. Overview

Webora ("we", "us", or "our") respects your personal and business privacy. This Privacy Policy clarifies what information we collect when you visit our website, submit inquiries, or contract our web development and design services.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information that you knowingly and voluntarily provide to us, such as:

  • Contact details: Name, corporate email address, phone number, and WhatsApp handle.
  • Project requirements: Brief notes, technical scope, brand assets, and budget parameters.
  • Technical telemetry: Anonymous, cookie-less website analytics (referrals, device class) used strictly for performance optimization.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your information exclusively to:

  • Evaluate project viability and formulate detailed proposals.
  • Execute design, development, and hosting deployment services under contract.
  • Communicate project milestones and support updates.

We never sell, rent, or trade client information to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Data Storage and Security

All client communications and deliverables are stored in encrypted environments. We retain project data only for the duration necessary to satisfy ongoing service contracts and warranty commitments.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please email us directly at hello@webora.com.
