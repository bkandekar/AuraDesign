Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

1. Milestone Based Protection

We work on a transparent milestone structure (usually 50% project commencement deposit and 50% upon completed staging approval before live DNS rollout). This ensures both parties are fully aligned at each stage.

2. Initial Design Phase Guarantee

If during Milestone 1 (Figma Interactive Wireframe & Design Prototype) you are not completely satisfied with our creative direction after up to 2 rounds of structural revisions, you may request a cancellation, and 50% of the initial deposit will be promptly refunded.

3. Final Code Delivery

Once final code approval is granted and the source code is deployed to your live server or GitHub repository, the engagement is deemed fulfilled and non-refundable. However, all our work is protected under our 30-day bug-free warranty.
