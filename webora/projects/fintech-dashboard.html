The Challenge

PayNova builds high-throughput payment APIs for multinational platforms. Despite having superior technology, their legacy marketing site suffered from confusing technical jargon, slow load speeds (4.2s), and high bounce rates among non-technical procurement executives.

Our Strategy & Execution

  • Two-Track Narrative: Designed high-level value propositions for CFOs while embedding interactive API snippet sandboxes for lead software architects.
  • Sub-Second Architecture: Rewrote the frontend into pure semantic HTML and Vanilla JS, reducing bundle size from 3.8MB to 42KB.
  • Frictionless Inbound Flow: Replaced the 12-field corporate form with an interactive 3-step qualification modal.
