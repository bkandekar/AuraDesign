The Vanilla Web Advantage: Why We Build Agency Sites Without Heavy Bundles

By Engineering Lead • Feb 27, 2026 • 6 min read

Modern frontend development often falls into the trap of over-engineering: compiling hundreds of megabytes of dependencies just to render simple marketing pages. The web platform itself has evolved dramatically.

CSS Custom Properties and Native Grid

With native CSS Grid, Flexbox, and CSS Variables, we achieve complex responsive layouts and dark/light theming without the runtime overhead of CSS-in-JS libraries.

Zero Build Step & Infinite Longevity

A pure HTML/CSS/JS website will run in every browser today, five years from now, and twenty years from now without broken dependency trees or security patch vulnerabilities.
