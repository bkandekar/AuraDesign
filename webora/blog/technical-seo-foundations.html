Technical SEO Checklist: What Developers Miss in Modern Semantic Markup

By SEO Strategist • Mar 29, 2026 • 8 min read

Search engines do not read websites the way humans do. While visitors perceive aesthetics and layout, crawlers parse DOM depth, heading semantics, canonical tags, and structured JSON-LD schemas.

1. Schema.org Structured Data

Implementing rich snippets (Organization, LocalBusiness, FAQPage, Article) directly inside the document head gives Google unambiguous signals for rich SERP features.

2. Semantic Landmark Tags

Use <header>, <nav>, <main>, <article>, <section>, and <footer> instead of generic nested <div> trees to maximize crawl budget efficiency.

3. Image Performance & Dimensions

Always specify explicit width and height attributes to eliminate Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS) penalties.
