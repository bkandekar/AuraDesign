Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This is a general-purpose template. Please review it with a qualified professional before publishing, and update it to reflect your actual business practices.

1. Introduction

Webora ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and protect information when you visit our website or contact us about a project.

2. Information we collect

We may collect your name, business name, email address, phone number and any project details you share with us through our contact form, cost estimator, or WhatsApp.

3. How we use your information

We use the information you provide to respond to enquiries, prepare quotes, deliver projects, and communicate with you about your website. We do not sell your information to third parties.

4. Cookies and local storage

Our website may use browser local storage to remember preferences such as your dark mode setting. We do not use this to track you across other websites.

5. Third-party services

Submitting our contact or estimator forms may open a conversation with us via WhatsApp, which is operated by Meta and subject to its own privacy policy.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us directly.

7. Contact us

For any privacy-related questions, please contact us at hello@webora.in.
